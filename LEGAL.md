# Legal notice and privacy

## Nature of the tool

IBD CAPO’S Lite is a **research prototype for decision support**. It is not a medical device, has no regulatory clearance and must not be used as the sole basis for a diagnosis or a treatment decision. Results are rule-based points, not probabilities. A qualified clinician remains responsible for every decision.

## Data processing

- **No server component.** The site is static. It sends no data anywhere.
- **No analytics, no cookies, no third-party request.** Fonts and images are served from this repository.
- **Diagnosis and score pages:** inputs exist only in the page’s memory. Reloading or closing the page erases them. The printed summary contains no name, identifier or contact.
- **Agenda page:** entries are saved in your browser’s local storage under the key `ibdcapos.lite.agenda.v1`, on your device only. Use the *Delete all appointments* button or clear your browser data to remove them. The agenda is a demonstration: **do not enter real patient information.**
- **Reminders:** the WhatsApp and email buttons open WhatsApp or your mail application with a prefilled message. The user sends it. The site sends nothing.

## Data used to build this prototype

| Source | Content | Included in this repository |
|---|---|---|
| Rule table of the IBD CAPO’S desktop application | Scoring rules (points per finding) | Yes, as `js/rules.js` |
| Synthetic test cases generated by `tests/make_reference.py` | Random combinations of findings, with results from the original code | Yes, `tests/reference.json` (no patient data) |
| Fictional agenda examples | Three “Demo Patient” entries, created on request | Created in the browser only |
| Real patient records | Clinical dataset of the author’s doctoral research | **No. No patient record, database or export is included.** |

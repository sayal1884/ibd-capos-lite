Guide

What this web version does, how to read its results, and where it stops.

What it is

IBD CAPO’S Lite is the web edition of a desktop expert system built for the differential diagnosis of inflammatory bowel diseases in settings with limited specialist resources. It was developed for research at the University of Yaoundé I, Cameroon.

How the differential diagnosis works

  • Each of seven conditions (Crohn’s disease, ulcerative colitis, intestinal tuberculosis, infectious colitis, radiation colitis, celiac disease, indeterminate colitis) receives points from the findings you tick, between 0 and 100.
  • The eighth line, Other diagnosis, takes the highest of eight non-inflammatory sub-scores: hemorrhoids, diverticulosis, dolichocolon/IBS, tumor/polyp, microscopic colitis, Hirschsprung disease, Behçet disease and a residual category.
  • The highest score is the leading diagnosis. Below 30 points the result is reported as inconclusive. Other diagnoses at or above half of the top score (and at least 30) are listed as secondary.
  • Open any line to see the findings that added or removed points.
  • Scores are rule-based points. They are not probabilities.

Reading the results

  • The ranking appears once at least three findings are ticked or chosen.
  • A list left on “Not specified” counts as “not present” in rules written as “not equal to”. Leaving Rectal bleeding and Alternating bowel habit unspecified therefore gives Radiation colitis a small baseline of 14 points. Choose the real value when you know it.
  • Age only affects the non-inflammatory sub-scores (for example diverticulosis, tumor/polyp and Hirschsprung disease).
  • “Print a summary” produces a page with the findings and the ranking, and no identifier. Save it as PDF from your browser’s print dialog.

Scores

  • CDAI: activity score computed from stools, pain, well-being, complications, antidiarrheal use, abdominal mass, hematocrit and weight. The formula used is shown with each result.
  • Marsh-Oberhuber: histological classification of the duodenal biopsy. If the combination you enter matches no category, the rules fall back to Marsh 0 and the page warns you.
  • CDEIS, SES-CD, UCEIS and Rutgeerts are available in the desktop application only.

Agenda

A demonstration of the appointment manager: calendar, conflict check within 30 minutes, reschedule, cancel, reminder messages through WhatsApp or your email application, and .ics export for your own calendar. It sends nothing itself.

Limits

  • The rules have been reviewed by a clinician but have not been validated in a prospective study.
  • The tool supports a clinician’s reasoning. It never replaces specialist assessment, endoscopy, histology or microbiology.
  • The endoscopic image analysis module (EndoVision CAPO’S) is part of the desktop application and is not included here.

Privacy

This site has no server component. Diagnosis and score inputs are held in memory and disappear when you close or reload the page. Agenda entries are kept in your browser’s local storage until you delete them. See LEGAL.md in the repository for details.
